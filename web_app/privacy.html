Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 July 2026

marketplace-store-fef91.web.app helps buyers discover businesses, products, and verified sellers across India. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what controls users have when using our marketplace, phone authentication, Google sign-in, and related services.

Information We Collect

  • Account details such as name, email address, phone number, selected role, and optional GSTIN.
  • Authentication data generated through Firebase Authentication, Google sign-in, and phone OTP verification.
  • Marketplace activity such as product views, saved items, inquiry actions, search terms, and preference signals used to improve recommendations.
  • Technical information such as device type, approximate traffic source, browser events, and limited analytics metadata.

How We Use Information

  • To create and maintain buyer and seller accounts.
  • To authenticate users through phone OTP and Google sign-in.
  • To show relevant suppliers, saved items, business information, and marketplace recommendations.
  • To improve trust, safety, fraud prevention, support, and service performance.

GST and Business Information

Seller GSTIN is optional in the current registration flow. If provided, it may be stored with the user profile and used to present verification-related marketplace trust signals. Users are responsible for submitting accurate business information.

Third-Party Services

We rely on third-party providers such as Firebase, Google Authentication services, Google reCAPTCHA, hosting infrastructure, and analytics integrations. These providers may process limited information required to deliver sign-in, security, and platform functionality.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited data with infrastructure, authentication, analytics, and fraud-prevention providers as required to operate the service. Seller-facing business information may be visible to buyers within the marketplace experience.

User Controls

  • Users can sign out at any time from the marketplace interface.
  • Users can control privacy toggles for analytics, personalization, and location features where provided.
  • Users may contact support to request account updates or deletion assistance.

Security

We use industry-standard controls such as HTTPS, security headers, authentication services, and access protections. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so users should also protect their own account credentials and devices.

Contact

For privacy questions, support, or data-related requests, contact the marketplace-store-fef91.web.app operator through the support channels published on the site.
