# Onboarding, Empty States, Accessibility

## Onboarding Flow (under 5 minutes)
1. Welcome and promise
2. Add business details
3. Add first product
4. Set response channel (WhatsApp and app)
5. See first insights card

## First-Run Checklist
- Business profile complete
- First product published
- Contact channel active
- Notification enabled
- Insights started

## Empty States
- Home:
  - Your dashboard will grow as buyers discover your products.
  - Action: Add first product
- My Products:
  - No products yet. Add your first item now.
  - Action: Add Product
- Messages:
  - No messages yet. Share your product to get inquiries.
  - Action: Share Listing
- Insights:
  - We need a few visits to show trends.
  - Action: Invite buyers

## Accessibility Requirements
- WCAG 2.2 AA target
- Minimum contrast ratio:
  - Normal text 4.5:1
  - Large text 3:1
- Keyboard navigation for all interactions
- Visible focus state for every control
- Hit target minimum 44x44
- ARIA labels for charts and icon-only buttons
- Reduced motion support
- Screen-reader friendly chart summaries in plain text

## Dark Mode
- Preserve contrast in status colors
- Keep accent usage minimal
- Avoid pure black backgrounds
