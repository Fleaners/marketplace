# UX Writing Guidelines

## Voice
- Friendly
- Clear
- Encouraging
- Human

## Tone
- New users: reassuring
- Active sellers: concise and action-focused
- Alerts: direct, calm, specific

## Replace Jargon With Plain Language
- Inventory Management -> My Products
- Analytics Dashboard -> Business Insights
- Conversion Metrics -> People Who Contacted You
- Revenue Trends -> Sales This Month

## Message Patterns
- Good news format:
  - Good news! Your {product} is trending this week.
- Suggestion format:
  - Suggestion: {small action} could improve inquiries.
- Alert format:
  - Inventory Alert: {product} may run out in {days} days.
- Insight format:
  - Customer Insight: Most visitors this week are {segment}.

## CTA Rules
- Start with verb
- Maximum 3 words where possible
- Examples:
  - Respond now
  - Add photos
  - Reorder stock
  - Share listing

## Error Copy
- Keep blame-free language
- Include one clear next step
- Example:
  - We could not upload your file. Please check the CSV format and try again.

## Empty State Copy
- Explain what this section is for
- Give one first action
- Example:
  - No products yet. Add your first product in less than two minutes.
