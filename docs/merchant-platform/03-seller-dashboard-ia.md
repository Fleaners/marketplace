# Seller Information Architecture

## Primary Navigation
1. Home
2. My Products
3. Customer Messages
4. Business Insights
5. Profile
6. Help Center

## Home (6 cards only)
1. Today's Visitors
2. Most Popular Product
3. New Customer Messages
4. Products Running Low
5. Business Growth
6. Recommended Action

## My Products
- Product card grid
- Add Product
- Upload CSV
- Import from Excel
- Quick Actions per card:
  - Edit
  - Pause
  - Share
  - Duplicate
  - Contact Buyers

## Customer Messages
- Priority inbox
- WhatsApp and in-app thread blending
- Suggested replies
- Response-time quality indicator

## Business Insights
- Story-first summaries:
  - People are interested in
  - Most visitors come from
  - Best time to respond
  - Products growing fastest
- Charts below stories:
  - Visitors this month (line)
  - Most viewed products (bar)
  - Category interest (pie)

## Profile
- Business details
- Verification status
- Team members
- Notification settings
- AI copilot preferences

## Help Center
- 1-click support chat
- Guided setup checklist
- Learning cards for beginners
